2. Where the prices come from, and their limits

Cash bids come from reports published by the USDA Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS) Livestock, Poultry & Grain Market News (mymarketnews.ams.usda.gov). Our own computer copies the numbers from those reports and puts them on this site as plain files. The app does not get prices from elevators.

  • They may be delayed. USDA posts reports about 2 to 4 pm Central on business days. The page shows the report date and when USDA posted it. Prices are not real-time.
  • They may be preliminary. Some reports are marked preliminary and can change. The app marks them “PRELIM”.
  • They may be old (stale). If a report is more than a day or two old, or our copy has not updated, the app tries to say so, but it may not catch everything.
  • They may be wrong or missing. USDA says price reporting is voluntary and reporters may leave out outliers. Our copying can also make mistakes (the reports are PDF files). A bid may be missing or in the wrong place.
  • They are regional bids. Most bids are for a region, not for one elevator, and are not offers to buy from you. A firm named on the Directory layer near a region’s pin is not necessarily part of that region’s report.

The optional Directory layer lists licensed grain firms from state lists; it contains names and towns only, no prices, and is not a source of any bid in this app.

3. Distances and regions are approximate

Distances are straight-line miles (as the crow flies), not driving miles. Most bids are for a region, so the pin is drawn at a point we chose (marked with “~”). USDA does not publish exact county lines for these regions, so a region’s distance and whether it falls inside the 300-mile circle are rough. A bid just outside the circle may be partly inside it. Do not use the app to plan a route, a freight cost or a delivery.

4. Futures settlements; no CME or USDA connection

Where the app shows futures closing settlement prices, they are copied as printed in the USDA AMS Market News reports, with credit to USDA AMS. They are not live quotes.
