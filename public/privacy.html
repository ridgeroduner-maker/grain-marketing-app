Privacy Policy

Effective date: [[EFFECTIVE DATE]] · Owner: Ridge Roduner (an individual) · Contact: ridgeroduner@gmail.com

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-up. We do not keep a database of users.
  • Your alerts, settings and location stay on your own phone or computer. They are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • The app uses no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • The map and all the app’s files come from this same site. The app does not call outside map, font, script or analytics services.
  • GitHub, which hosts this site on GitHub Pages (see below), may keep its own server logs, such as your IP address. That is outside the app.

1. What we collect

Nothing, through the app itself. The app has no sign-in, no forms that send anything to a server, no user database, and no code that sends your alerts, location or settings anywhere. The only things the app asks the network for are its own files and the price data files from this site, plus the map file on this site.

2. What is saved on your device

The app saves a few things in your browser, on your device only. We cannot see them.

  • Price alerts you create (the commodity, place, price and direction): saved in the browser’s local storage, plus a copy in the browser’s IndexedDB so the app’s background worker can check them.
  • Your location, only if you tap “Use my location” (see next section).
  • A saved copy of the app and the latest prices in the browser’s cache, so the app opens faster and can show the last prices when you are offline (the app then tells you the data is old). The map file is not saved for offline use.

This is not a cookie. To remove it all, clear this site’s data in your browser settings, or remove the installed app. Deleting an alert in the app removes that alert.

3. Your location

The app only asks for your location when you tap “Use my location”. Your browser then asks your permission, and you can say no (the app then uses Gann Valley, SD as the starting point). With your permission, the app reads your approximate position once and uses it only inside your browser to work out straight-line distances to each bid and to show a dot on the map. It is not sent to us, to USDA, or to any map company. The app remembers it on your device (local storage) so you do not have to tap again; clear this site’s data to remove it. You can also turn location off for this site in your browser or phone settings.

4. Notifications

The app asks your browser for permission to show notifications only when you tap “Enable notifications”. Alerts are worked out on your device when you open the app (about every 15 minutes while it is open), and on some installed apps the browser may run a background check. No server sends you messages, and no push service is used.

5. Cookies, analytics and outside services

  • No cookies. The app does not set or read cookies.
  • No analytics, advertising, social-media buttons or tracking pixels.
  • No outside fonts, scripts, libraries or map servers. The map (OpenStreetMap-based data from Protomaps), the map and chart software, the icons and the price files are all served from this site.
  • The links on the pages (for example to USDA or OpenStreetMap) only open if you tap them. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

6. The company that hosts this site

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub may log requests when your device asks for a page, such as your IP address, the date and time, the page asked for, and your browser and device type. GitHub keeps and uses those logs under its own policy, not this one: see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The app itself does not receive or store those logs. 7. Sharing, selling and keeping data

We do not have your personal data, so we do not sell it, rent it or share it. Data saved on your device stays there until you clear it.

8. Children

The app is for people who farm or work in grain markets and is not aimed at children. It does not ask for personal information. [[LAWYER: confirm any children’s-privacy wording]]

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. See also our Terms of Use. Questions: ridgeroduner@gmail.com
