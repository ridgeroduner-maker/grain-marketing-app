Price alerts

    How alerts work (and their limits)

    Alerts are stored only in this browser (localStorage / IndexedDB). They are checked every time you open the app and about every 15 minutes while it is open. USDA reports are posted once per business day (about 2 to 4 pm Central), so a given alert can only fire once per day's report. Alerts ignore bids more than 2 business days old.

    No server = no true push. Web Push needs a push service plus a server that sends messages, so a closed app cannot be woken on a schedule by this version. On Chrome/Edge (Android & desktop) an installed app may also get Periodic Background Sync checks (experimental, browser-throttled, not guaranteed). iOS needs the app added to the Home Screen (iOS 16.4+) and only supports notifications while using a push server.

    Source: USDA Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS) Livestock, Poultry & Grain Market News — reports SD 3186, ND 3878, NE 3225, S. MN 3049, IA 2850, sunflowers 2887, at mymarketnews.ams.usda.gov. Reports are posted about 2 to 4 pm Central each business day; the “Posted” time is when USDA published each one. Bids are by region (not by elevator), except sunflowers, which USDA lists by processor town. This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by USDA.

    About USDA’s numbers: USDA says price reporting is voluntary, reporters may exclude outliers, and basis may be figured from the current or the prior day’s close (paraphrased — see USDA’s report pages for its official wording). Futures settlements, when shown, are copied as printed in those USDA reports; they are not live quotes.

    Calculated by this app, not by USDA: the “high end of range” used for ranking, “best”, straight-line distances, map pin positions (region pins are approximate), cents-to-dollars conversion of settlements, the exchange named on each basis (CBOT for corn and soybeans, MGE for spring wheat, KCBT for winter wheat), and changes since the previous report we loaded.
