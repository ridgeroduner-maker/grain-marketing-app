What do these words mean?
Cash bid
The price a buyer is posting for grain. In this app, that number comes from a USDA regional report, not a quote from one elevator. Always call to confirm.
Grade and haul
Grade is the quality of the grain (for example US #2 Yellow corn). Haul, sometimes called freight, is who trucks it: Delivered means the posted price is for grain brought to the buyer; FOB means you haul it yourself.
Basis
How far the cash price is above or below the futures market, in cents. A basis of −40¢ means the cash price is 40 cents under the futures price named in the report.
When / delivery
Current means you can deliver now. Other choices are later delivery periods printed in the USDA report.
Miles
Straight-line miles (as the crow flies) from you, or from Gann Valley if you have not shared your location. This is not driving distance.

Price alerts

Get a notice on this device when a USDA regional bid reaches a price you set.

    How alerts work (and their limits)

    Alerts are stored only in this browser (localStorage / IndexedDB). They are checked every time you open the app and about every 15 minutes while it is open. USDA reports are posted once per business day (about 2 to 4 pm Central), so a given alert can only fire once per day's report. Alerts ignore any bid from a report more than 1 business day old, the same bids the app leaves out of its rankings and warns you not to rely on.

    No server = no true push. Web Push needs a push service plus a server that sends messages, so a closed app cannot be woken on a schedule by this version. On Chrome/Edge (Android & desktop) an installed app may also get Periodic Background Sync checks (experimental, browser-throttled, not guaranteed). iOS needs the app added to the Home Screen (iOS 16.4+) and only supports notifications while using a push server.

    Source: USDA Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS) Livestock, Poultry & Grain Market News — reports SD 3186, ND 3878, NE 3225, S. MN 3049, IA 2850, sunflowers 2887, at mymarketnews.ams.usda.gov. Reports are posted about 2 to 4 pm Central each business day; the “Posted” time is when USDA published each one. Bids are by region (not by elevator), except sunflowers, which USDA lists by processor town. Grain Bids is not affiliated with or endorsed by USDA, CME Group, any state agency, or any company named in the Directory.

    About USDA’s numbers: USDA says price reporting is voluntary, reporters may exclude outliers, and basis may be figured from the current or the prior day’s close (paraphrased — see USDA’s report pages for its official wording). Futures settlements, when shown, are copied as printed in those USDA reports; they are not live quotes.

    Calculated by this app, not by USDA: the “high end of range” used for ranking, “best”, straight-line distances, map pin positions (region pins are approximate), cents-to-dollars conversion of settlements, the exchange named on each basis (CBOT for corn and soybeans, MGE for spring wheat, KCBT for winter wheat), and changes since the previous report we loaded. Call your elevator to confirm any price. Not trading advice.
